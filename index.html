Our Culinary Story

MITH Thai Cuisine offers a traditional Thai dining experience in the heart of Geelong West. Guests can enjoy a variety of classic Thai dishes such as Pad Thai Goong Sod, Khao Pad Kratiem Gai Tod, and Ba Mhee Moo Palo.

Our menu also features hearty mains including Nuer Toon Pad Kaprao and Beef Gaeng Massaman, which are particularly popular among patrons. Highly praised for reflecting the rich and authentic flavours of Thailand.
